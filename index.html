    06 / FAQ

    Questions,
    answered.

    How long does a website take?+

    The timeline depends on the number of pages, content, and features. A focused business site can be built much faster than a large custom platform.

    Can you make the website mobile-friendly?+

    Yes. Mobile is part of the build rather than an afterthought. Layouts, navigation, buttons, typography, and media are adapted for smaller screens.

    Can you connect my domain?+

    Yes. Once the site is ready, the domain can be connected through DNS and HTTPS can be enabled.

    Can you update the site later?+

    Yes. ESC can continue improving the site, adding pages, changing content, and building new functionality as the business grows.

    Do I need to know how to code?+

    No. The technical work is handled as part of the website build. You provide the business information, content, images, and direction.
